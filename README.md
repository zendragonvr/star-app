# Star Bridge

**Submission for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon — Indie Developer track, "Build for your world."**

## What existed before

Nothing. The idea began on October 26, 2026, after the hackathon kickoff.

## What it is, in plain words

Star Bridge is a mixed-reality app for Meta Quest headsets. You look at the real sky through passthrough, pinch a star, planet, or the Moon, and the app tells you what it is. An AI teacher answers your questions out loud. You can also travel to a planet's surface, and spawn a Starship.

It is for people who are curious about the universe and want to learn about it — future scientists, future astrophysicists, anyone who looks up and wants to know what they are seeing.

## Try it

1. Open the app on a Meta Quest 3 / 3S. Allow the microphone and precise location, and connect the headset to the internet (a phone hotspot works).
2. Look at the real sky. Pinch a star or planet to bring it to you, with its name and description.
3. Say "Star Bridge, show me Mars" — or any planet, star, or the Moon.
4. Say "Star Bridge, take me to Mars" to travel to that world. Any pinch brings you back.
5. Ask the teacher anything. It is a space specialist, but you can ask it any question.
6. Say "Star Bridge, spawn starship" to place a Starship, then "launch starship" to launch it.

## The hard problem, and how it was solved

A Quest has no GPS and no compass, so the app cannot tell, on its own, which way you are facing in the real sky.

Star Bridge solves this with Sky Sync, done once when you open the app:

- In the morning, open the STAR BRIDGE menu (left palm facing you, then pinch) and choose SYNC SUN. Put the ring on the real Sun and pinch. The whole sky turns to match.
- At night, choose SYNC MOON instead, and put the ring on the real Moon.

Planet and star positions are then calculated in real time from time and location, using published astronomy data, including NASA sources. Once synced, the whole solar system is aligned.

## Controls

- **Pinch:** identify a star, planet, or the Moon. Pinch a brought object again to send it back.
- **Voice:** "Star Bridge, show me [planet/star/Moon]", "Star Bridge, take me to [planet]", "sync Sun", "sync Moon", "spawn starship", "launch starship".
- **Menu:** left palm facing you, then pinch, opens the STAR BRIDGE menu: SYNC MOON, SYNC SUN, AI TEACHER.
- **Hands first:** the app is designed around hand tracking and voice, not controllers or buttons.

## What is in this build

- Real-sky star and planet identification by pinch, in passthrough
- AI teacher with spoken answers
- Sky Sync (Sun / Moon) for real-sky alignment without GPS or compass
- Travel worlds: Mars, the Moon, Saturn's rings, the Sun's surface, and more
- Starship: voice-placed, 10x real size outdoors, with launch, flight, and tower catch
- Zodiac: "Star Bridge, show me [sign]" shows that constellation in its real sky position for 60 seconds, with a spoken reading
- ISS: a bright sky point when it is really above the horizon, plus a brought model and Destiny interior

## How it is built

- Godot 4.3, GL Compatibility renderer, OpenXR, hand tracking, passthrough
- Teacher answers come from Muse Spark through the Meta Model API, via a small relay service. Voice transcription uses Meta Muse Voice. Spoken playback of teacher answers is generated by the relay (see CREDITS.md for the full, honest breakdown).
- Sounds in the app are the author's own synthesis, except where CREDITS.md says otherwise.
- No API keys are stored in this repository. The relay service holds its own secret; see `.env.example` in a full submission package.

## Build and install

1. Open this project in Godot 4.3.
2. Export with the Android / Quest preset (JDK 17, Android SDK, OpenXR enabled).
3. Install the APK on a Quest 3 / 3S with `adb install` or SideQuest, with Developer Mode on.
4. Allow microphone and precise location before first use, and connect to the internet for voice and teacher answers.
