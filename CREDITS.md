# Credits — Star Bridge

## Teacher and voice — honest breakdown

- Teacher answer text: Muse Spark, through the Meta Model API, via a small relay service. The relay holds its own secret; no API key is stored in this repository.
- Voice transcription: Meta Muse Voice.
- Spoken playback of teacher answers: generated by the relay. That spoken voice is not a Meta model — it is Google Translate text-to-speech, used for playback only.

## Astronomy data and imagery

- Planet and star positions use published astronomy data, including NASA sources.
- Planet textures are NASA public-domain imagery, used with credit. No NASA logo is used, and nothing here implies NASA endorsement.
- Travel worlds that are not photographic are labelled in the app as simulated or artistic.

## Sounds

- Sounds in the app are the author's own synthesis, except the telescope zoom sound, which is the YleArkisto "Kaitafilmikamera, zoomaus" recording (Canon 8mm, Freesound 335351), CC BY 4.0, credited here as required.

## Starship

- The Starship model is an original, unofficial representation. Not affiliated with SpaceX. No SpaceX logos, lettering, or branding are used anywhere in the app.
